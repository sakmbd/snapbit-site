Five tools, one icon

Everything for the small jobs you do every day.

Click the Snapbit icon in your toolbar and choose Capture, Color or Text. Everything runs in your browser.

Screenshot Capture

Save exactly what you need from any regular web page and use it right away.

  • Visible Area – what you see in the tab, at your screen's real resolution.
  • Selected Area – drag a rectangle on the page; the size is shown as you drag.
  • Full Page – the page is scrolled and stitched into one image, with sticky headers shown once.
  • Download as PNG or JPEG, copy to the clipboard, or open it in the Image Editor.

Image Editor

Explain what matters and hide what is private before you share.

  • Crop, rotate, flip and resize.
  • Draw freehand, add shapes, arrows and text labels.
  • Highlighter in five shades or a custom color.
  • Blur or pixelate private details; the effect is baked into the exported pixels.
  • Undo and redo, zoom and pan, keyboard shortcuts.
  • Open PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF files, or paste an image with Ctrl+V.

Screen Recorder

Record a tab, a window or your entire screen, then download it as a WebM video.

  • Choose the source in Chrome's own share dialog.
  • Tab audio, system audio where available (entire screen on Windows and ChromeOS) and your microphone.
  • 3-2-1 countdown, pause and resume; the countdown is not in the video.
  • Preview, then download as WebM (VP9 or VP8 with Opus audio).

Color Picker

Grab any color on your screen with the browser's native eyedropper.

  • HEX, RGB and HSL values, each with its own copy button.
  • The HEX code is copied automatically.
  • Works on web pages, photos and other apps (Chrome 95 or later).

Text Case Converter

Switch text between everyday and developer formats in one click.

  • Sentence case, lower case, UPPER CASE and Capitalized Case.
  • camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case and CONSTANT_CASE.
  • Handles accented and non-Latin letters; removes extra spaces; live character, word and line counts.
  • Select text on a web page, right-click and choose "Convert case with Snapbit".

FAQ

Questions and answers

What is Snapbit?

Snapbit is a free Chrome extension that helps you capture and reuse screen and page content. It combines Screenshot Capture, an Image Editor, a Screen Recorder, a Color Picker and a Text Case Converter in one toolbar icon.

Is Snapbit free?

Yes. Snapbit is free, with no premium plan, no locked features, no watermarks, no ads and no account.

Can Snapbit capture a full web page?

Yes. Full Page mode scrolls the page and stitches it into one image. Captures stop at 30,000 pixels in height, and pages that scroll inside an inner panel cannot be stitched.

Can I blur or pixelate sensitive information?

Yes. The Image Editor's Blur & Pixelate tool hides passwords, emails and other details, and the effect is applied to the exported pixels.

Which image formats can the editor open?

PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF (a GIF opens as its first frame), up to 100 MB and 60 megapixels. You can also paste an image with Ctrl+V.

Can I record a tab, a window or the whole screen?

Yes. Chrome's share dialog lets you choose a tab, a window or the entire screen. Tab audio is available for tabs, system audio for an entire screen on Windows and ChromeOS, and the microphone is optional.

What video format does the Screen Recorder use?

WebM (VP9 or VP8 video with Opus audio). There is no MP4 export.

Which text case formats are supported?

Sentence case, lower case, UPPER CASE, Capitalized Case, camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case and CONSTANT_CASE.

Does Snapbit upload my screenshots or recordings?

No. Screenshots, edited images and recordings are processed in your browser. Snapbit has no analytics, tracking or advertising code.

Where are screenshots and editor images stored?

Locally, in the extension's own storage in your Chrome profile. Editor copies expire after 24 hours and Clear Image deletes one immediately. Recordings stay in memory until you download them.

Are there pages Snapbit cannot work on?

Yes. Extensions cannot run on built-in Chrome pages or the Chrome Web Store, so some tools are limited there.

Was Snapbit called something else before?

Yes. Snapbit was previously called Convert 2 Case, and its text case converter is still included.
