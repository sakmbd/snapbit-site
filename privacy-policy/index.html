Snapbit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Snapbit is a free Chrome extension that helps you capture and reuse screen and page content: screenshots, an image editor, a screen recorder, a color picker and a text case converter. This policy explains what data Snapbit handles and what happens to it.

Summary

  • Snapbit processes everything locally in your browser.
  • Snapbit does not upload, sell or share your data, and sends nothing to any server.
  • Snapbit has no analytics, tracking, ads or accounts.

Data Snapbit handles

Snapbit handles data only when you start one of its tools:

ToolWhat it handlesWhere it goes
ScreenshotsAn image of the tab you are on (visible area, a selected area, or the full page)Shown in the extension; your latest screenshot is stored in the extension's local storage (IndexedDB) on your device
Image editorScreenshots you edit, and image files you open or pasteEditor copies are stored locally on your device
Screen recorderThe tab, window or screen you choose in Chrome's share dialog, plus tab or system audio and your microphone if you enable themKept in memory until you download the video; never written to storage by Snapbit
Color pickerThe color value of the pixel you clickShown in the extension and copied to your clipboard
Text case converterText you type or paste, or text you select on a page and send with "Convert case with Snapbit"Converted in the extension; passed to the popup through temporary in-memory session storage

Snapbit cannot read web pages in the background. It has no access to a page until you click its toolbar icon or its right-click menu item on that page, and that access is temporary.

Storage and deletion

  • Latest screenshot: kept until you delete it, take a new screenshot, or open the Capture tool after it has expired (30 minutes, or after the browser or extension restarts).
  • Editor copies: expire after 24 hours and are deleted the next time you open an image in the editor; Clear Image deletes one immediately.
  • Recordings: stay in memory only; closing the preview tab without downloading discards the video.
  • Settings: the recorder remembers your last audio choices (two on/off settings).
  • Session data (screenshot progress, recorder status, selected text being passed to the popup) lives in Chrome's in-memory session storage and is cleared when the browser closes.
  • Removing the extension removes the data Chrome stores for it.

Files you download (PNG, JPEG, WebM) and content you copy are saved by your browser and operating system, not by Snapbit.

Sharing and third parties

Snapbit does not transmit any data off your device, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not use data for advertising, creditworthiness or any purpose unrelated to its features.

Limited Use

The use of information received from Chrome APIs by Snapbit adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If Snapbit's data handling changes, this policy will be updated and the date above changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact shown on Snapbit's Chrome Web Store listing.
